================================================================================
  CROC 2 ARCHIPELAGO RANDOMIZER — INSTALLATION GUIDE
================================================================================

Welcome to the Croc 2 Archipelago Randomizer!
Follow these simple instructions to install the mod and start playing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 1: AUTOMATIC ONE-CLICK INSTALL (RECOMMENDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Run "Croc2AP_Setup.exe".
2. It will automatically detect your Croc 2 Steam directory and Archipelago directory.
3. Click "Install / Update Croc 2 AP".
4. Done! You can click "Launch Croc 2 AP Launcher" or open the shortcut
   created on your Desktop to jump right in.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 2: ONE-CLICK BATCH SCRIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click "Install_Croc2AP.bat" and select "Run as administrator".
2. The script will automatically copy all mod files, the .apworld, and the
   YAML template to their respective folders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 3: MANUAL INSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you prefer to place the files yourself:

1. Croc 2 Steam Folder:
   (Default: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Croc 2)
   Copy these files into your Croc 2 folder:
   - version.dll                  -> In-game hook and TCP state bridge
   - croc2_mod.ini                -> Mod configuration file
   - Croc2ArchipelagoLauncher.exe  -> Standalone UI launcher

2. Archipelago Installation:
   (Default: C:\ProgramData\Archipelago)
   - Copy "croc2.apworld" to:
     C:\ProgramData\Archipelago\custom_worlds\croc2.apworld
   - Copy "Croc 2.yaml" to:
     C:\ProgramData\Archipelago\Players\Templates\Croc 2.yaml

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO PLAY & GENERATE SEEDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CONFIGURE YOUR SETTINGS:
   - Open "Croc2ArchipelagoLauncher.exe" and switch to Tab 2:
     "Randomizer Settings & YAML Export".
   - Adjust your preferred Goal, White Crystals Granularity, Shop Shuffle,
     Door Shuffle, Abilities, Stage Locks, and Traps.
   - Click "Save & Export YAML to Archipelago Players" (or manually copy
     "Croc 2.yaml" into C:\ProgramData\Archipelago\Players\<YourName>.yaml).

2. GENERATE YOUR MULTIWORLD SEED:
   - Run ArchipelagoGenerate.exe (or ArchipelagoServer.exe / web host).

3. CONNECT & PLAY:
   - Launch Croc 2 (or click "Launch Croc 2" in the launcher).
   - In "Croc2ArchipelagoLauncher.exe" (Tab 1), enter your server host:port
     (e.g., archipelago.gg:38281) and your Slot Name.
   - Click "Connect". The launcher will automatically bridge with Croc 2,
     send your checks, grant items, and trigger trap toasts!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USEFUL CONTROLS & UNINSTALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Press F10 in-game at any time to toggle between Archipelago Mod and Vanilla Mode.
- To uninstall, run "Uninstall_Croc2AP.bat" or delete "version.dll" from your Croc 2 folder.
================================================================================
