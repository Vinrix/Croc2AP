# Croc 2

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

In the Croc 2 Archipelago Randomizer:
- **Abilities & Actions**: Croc's core moves (`Butt Stomp`, `Tail Whack`, `Climbing Claws`, `Power Gloves` for picking up objects) as well as `Lever Wrench`, `Vehicle License`, and `Airplane Ammo Feed` can be shuffled into the multiworld pool.
- **Colored Crystals & Golden Gobbos**: All 5 Colored Crystals per stage (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple) are shuffled into the multiworld pool by default. Receiving all 5 crystals for a stage automatically activates that stage's **Golden Gobbo**! Standard non-Golden Gobbos remain vanilla in-level platforming objectives by default.
- **Swap Meet Pete's General Store**: Entering Pete's tent can require a Village-specific `Pete's VIP Pass`, and Pete's shop inventory is randomized into multiworld location checks.
- **Stage Locks & Door Shuffle**: Level doors can require Level Unlock Keys and can be shuffled within each Village Hub or across all 4 Village Hubs.
- **Traps**: Includes the **Fire Breakout Trap** (3 burning ticks on Croc's tail that can be extinguished immediately by performing a **front flip**!), Ice Physics, Reverse Controls, Dantini Ambush, Speed Distortion, and Health Drain.
- **Zero-Friction Vanilla Toggle**: Play 100% vanilla Croc 2 anytime without removing files—the mod starts in Dormant Passthrough Mode whenever `Croc2Client` is not connected, and can also be toggled via `F10`, `croc2_mod.ini`, or `-vanilla`.
